# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Internal team of ~16 people across multiple services. Primary users are sales team members, the CEO, and other non-writer professionals who need to build their LinkedIn presence to book meetings and get content out. They are busy, not dedicated content creators, and need a tool that makes them better at posting without requiring writing skill or time investment.

## Product Purpose

Elevateo Posts: an Elevateo Co. internal LinkedIn post creation tool that guides users through discovering their unique voice, finding high-performing post inspiration, and generating authentic drafts they can refine with AI assistance. Success looks like: team members posting consistently on LinkedIn with content that sounds like them, not like a template. The tool should make the hardest part (figuring out what to say and how to say it) feel effortless.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, confident, fast, premium. Executive energy with no fluff. Carries the Elevateo Co. identity: gold-on-charcoal, restrained, expensive-feeling without being flashy. Feels like a tool built by someone who actually posts well on LinkedIn. Respects the user's time and intelligence. Never patronizing, never cheesy.

## Anti-references

- **Content mill tools** (Jasper, Copy.ai): feels automated, templated, impersonal. Our output should feel human-written, not generated.
- **Generic SaaS dashboards**: cookie-cutter admin panels with blue accents, card grids, sidebar nav. We're a focused tool, not an everything-platform.
- **Social media schedulers** (Buffer, Hootsuite): cluttered, feature-heavy, calendar-focused. We do one thing well.
- **AI chat interfaces** (ChatGPT-style): blank chat box that doesn't guide the user. Our tool should lead the user through a clear workflow, not drop them in an empty room.

## Design Principles

1. **One thing at a time.** Each screen has one job. No dashboards, no multi-panel layouts. Guide the user through a linear flow and get out of the way.
2. **Sound like a person, not a platform.** Every piece of copy, every label, every prompt should feel like advice from a sharp colleague, not enterprise software.
3. **Speed is respect.** The user is a busy exec or salesperson. Every interaction should feel instant. Minimize clicks, eliminate loading states where possible, default to the smart choice.
4. **Coach, don't automate.** The tool helps users find their voice and refine their thinking. It doesn't just spit out content. The onboarding, suggestions, and editor should all make the user better, not dependent.
5. **Confidence through constraint.** Fewer options, stronger defaults. A focused tool that makes decisions so the user doesn't have to agonize.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific requirements beyond building it well. Standard contrast ratios, keyboard navigable, semantic HTML. Internal tool with no known accessibility needs on the team.
